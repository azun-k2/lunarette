What it does

Lunarette puts today's lunar day in your menu bar, in a small moon or written out. Click it for the month with every lunar day, the festivals and the 24 solar terms.

  • Four traditions: Chinese, Vietnamese, Korean and Japanese, each reckoned at its own meridian as its published calendar is. Now and then a month really does begin a day apart, and Lunarette gets it right: Tết 1985, Seollal 2027.
  • Every day in full: the lunar date the way your tradition writes it (丙午年八月十五, Ngày 15 tháng 8 năm Bính Ngọ, 병오년 8월 15일), the day's and month's stems and branches, the year's animal, and Japan's rokuyō.
  • Festivals and solar terms: the Spring Festival, Tết, Seollal, the Lantern Festival, Qingming, the Dragon Boat Festival, Vu Lan, Mid-Autumn, Chuseok and more, with a countdown to the next ones.
  • A date converter: solar to lunar and back, leap months included, ready to copy.
  • Lunar reminders: an anniversary on its lunar date every year, or every 1st and 15th, arriving on the right solar day each time.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Covers 1901 to 2099.

Privacy in one paragraph

Lunarette has no network access, no account and no tracking. The calendar is worked out on your Mac from where the Moon and the Sun are, and your reminders stay on your Mac. The full privacy policy says the same at more length.
