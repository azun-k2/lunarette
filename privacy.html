The short version

Lunarette collects nothing, sends nothing and has no network access.

What is stored, and where

Inside the app's own sandbox container on your Mac: your settings and your reminders, with the names you give them. Deleting the app removes them.

Lunarette asks macOS for permission to show notifications when you save your first reminder. The notifications are created on your Mac and are not sent anywhere.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Lunarette has no network entitlement, which means macOS itself will not let it open a network connection. The calendar is calculated on your Mac. There is no server, no analytics and no account.
