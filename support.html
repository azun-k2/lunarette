Common questions

Why does my calendar differ by a day from another one?

The Chinese calendar is reckoned at Beijing's meridian, the Vietnamese at Hanoi's, and the Korean and Japanese at 135° east. When a new moon falls close to midnight, the month begins on different days. Choose your tradition in Settings ▸ Calendar.

How are leap months handled?

A lunar year with thirteen months repeats one of them; Lunarette follows the same rule as the published calendars. In the converter, a Leap month box appears when the year you choose has one.

My reminder is on the 30th, but the month has 29 days

Then it arrives on the 29th, as families usually keep it. A yearly reminder is kept in the regular month, not the leap one.

Reminders do not arrive

Allow notifications for Lunarette in System Settings ▸ Notifications, and check that a Focus is not holding them back. Settings ▸ Reminders tells you when notifications are turned off.
